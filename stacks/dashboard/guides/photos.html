📷 Family Photos

Our photos back up to our own private cloud, called Immich — like Google Photos, but it's ours and stays home.

Get the app & auto-backup

  1. Install Immich from the App Store or Play Store.
  2. Open it. Server URL: https://immich.kmkdp.com
  3. Sign in with your house account (ask Jacob to create one).
  4. Turn on Backup and pick your Camera Roll. From then on, new photos back up automatically when you're on Wi-Fi.

Browse, search & share

  • Search by person, place, or thing ("beach", "dog", "birthday").
  • Make albums and share them with the family.
  • See everything in a browser too: immich.kmkdp.com.
