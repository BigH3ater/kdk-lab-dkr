💵 Budget

Our household budget lives in Actual — a simple, private budgeting app that stays on our own server.

Sign in

  1. Go to budget.kmkdp.com.
  2. Choose Sign in with Authelia and use your kmkdp house account.
  3. Open the household budget file.
Open the Budget

The basics

  • Each month, money is given a job across categories (groceries, gas, fun, savings).
  • As you spend, transactions come in and you see what's left in each category.
  • There's a phone app too — install Actual and point it at https://budget.kmkdp.com.
