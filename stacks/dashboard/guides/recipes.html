🍽️ Dinner & Recipes

The week's dinners live right on the home screen. This is where you see what's for supper, open the recipe, and tell us if it was a keeper.

See what's for dinner

Open the home screen. The This Week box at the top shows each night's meal. Tap a meal to open the full recipe.

Rate a meal

Two easy ways:

  1. Quick stars on the home screen: under each meal just tap the stars — the dashboard already knows who's signed in.
  2. On the recipe page: tap the meal to open it in the recipe app and use the star rating there. This one is saved to your account.

If we made something and everyone loved it (or nobody did), rating it helps us plan better weeks. No wrong answers!

Browse recipes & the shopping list

The full recipe book is at recipes.kmkdp.com.

  • Search recipes, see ingredients, and adjust servings.
  • Add things to the shopping list — it already knows what's in the pantry, so it only lists what we actually need.

First time? Tap Sign in with Authelia with your house login. Once you're in, you'll see the "Mack House" recipes.
