📚 Books & eBooks

Request a book, and read it on the reMarkable tablet or in a reading app. Here's how both parts work.

Request a book

  1. Go to libreseerr.kmkdp.com and sign in with your house account.
  2. Search for the book by title or author.
  3. Tap Request.
Request a Book

Read on the reMarkable

Books you request are added to the tablet's library automatically. When the reMarkable is on Wi-Fi, it pulls in new books on its own — just open it and look in your books.

Read on a phone, tablet, or e-reader

All our eBooks are also in a Calibre library you can open on the home network (or over Tailscale when you're out).

  • In a browser: open http://10.1.20.20:8081 to read online.
  • In a reading app (like KOReader): add this as an OPDS catalog: http://10.1.20.20:8081/opds
