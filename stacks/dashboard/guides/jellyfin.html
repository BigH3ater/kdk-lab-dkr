📺 Watch Movies & TV

All our movies and shows are on our own private Netflix, called Jellyfin. Watch on the big TV or on your phone.

On the Apple TV (best picture)

The TV app is called Neptune.

  1. On the Apple TV, open the App Store and search Neptune (a Jellyfin player). Install it.
  2. Open Neptune. When it asks for the server, enter https://jellyfin.kmkdp.com
  3. Sign in with your house account (ask Jacob for a login if you don't have one).
  4. Pick a movie or show and hit play. It remembers where you left off.

On your phone or iPad

  1. Install Neptune (or the official Jellyfin app) from the App Store.
  2. Server: https://jellyfin.kmkdp.com, then sign in.

Or just open jellyfin.kmkdp.com in a web browser — no app needed.

Can't find something?

If a movie or show isn't in the library, you can request it — see Request a Movie or Show. It usually shows up within a day.

No sound, or it won't play? Text Jacob — that's usually a quick fix on his end.
