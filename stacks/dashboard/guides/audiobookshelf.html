🎧 Audiobooks

Our audiobooks live in Audiobookshelf. Listen on your phone in the car, on a walk, or anywhere.

Get the app

  1. Install Audiobookshelf from the App Store (iPhone) or Play Store (Android).
  2. Open it. For the server address, enter https://audiobookshelf.kmkdp.com
  3. Sign in with your house account (ask Jacob if you need one).

Listen

  • Tap any book to start. It keeps your place across all your devices.
  • Tap the download icon to save a book on your phone for offline listening (great for flights or spotty signal).
  • Adjust the speed, set a sleep timer, and skip forward/back from the player.

Prefer the web? Open audiobookshelf.kmkdp.com in a browser.
