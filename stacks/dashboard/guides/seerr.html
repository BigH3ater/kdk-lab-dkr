➕ Request a Movie or Show

Want to watch something we don't have yet? Just ask for it and it'll be added automatically.

How to request

  1. Go to seerr.kmkdp.com.
  2. Sign in with your Jellyfin login (same one you use to watch).
  3. Search for the movie or show.
  4. Tap Request. For a show, you can ask for a whole series or just certain seasons.
Open Requests

What happens next

  • Pending — we got your request.
  • Processing / Downloading — it's on the way.
  • Available — it's ready! Open Jellyfin and watch.

Most things are ready within a few hours to a day. Newer movies still in theaters can take longer.
