## 🎞 Tips for the best picture

1. **On a TV, use an Ethernet cable if you can.** Plug the TV or Apple TV into your router or a wall port with a network (Ethernet) cable. Wi-Fi works too, but a cable means no stutter in 4K.
2. **Quality setting**: in the app's playback settings choose **Automatic** or **Maximum**. If a movie keeps pausing to load, lower it one step.
3. **Subtitles and audio**: press the subtitle or audio button in the player to change the language.
4. **Something not working?** Say which app, which device and what you saw on the screen, and we will sort it out.

*Enjoy!* 🍿
