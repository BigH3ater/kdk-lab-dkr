## 📺 Step 2: Install the app for your device

Use **only these apps**. They are the ones we support and have tested. In every app the server address is:

**https://jellyfin.kmkdp.com**

|||
### 🍎 Apple TV, iPhone, iPad
**Neptune Media Player** from the App Store.

1. Install Neptune, open it, choose **Add server** and type the address above.
2. Sign in with your **username and password**.
   (Do not use "Quick Connect" to add your account on the Apple TV; it does not finish setup yet.)
3. On a shared Apple TV, each person adds their own account.

[Neptune on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/neptune-media-player/id6756797773){target=_blank}
|||

|||
### 🤖 Android phone, tablet or Android TV
**Jellyfin** (the official app, blue icon) from Google Play.

1. Install Jellyfin, open it, type the server address above.
2. Sign in with your username and password.

[Jellyfin on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.jellyfin.mobile){target=_blank}
|||

|||
### 📺 Roku
**Jellyfin** from the Roku Channel Store (search for "Jellyfin").

1. Add the channel, open it, choose **Add server** and type the address above.
2. Sign in with your username and password.
|||

|||
### 🌐 Web browser (any computer)
Go to **https://jellyfin.kmkdp.com** and click **Sign in with SSO**. It uses the sign-in you set up in Step 1. Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox all work.
