## 🔑 Forgot your password?

You can reset it yourself in two minutes. The same password is used by all the apps.

1. Open **https://sso.kmkdp.com** and click **Reset password?** under the sign-in box.
2. Type your **username** and press Send.
3. Check your email for a message from **no-reply@kmkdp.com** (look in Spam or Junk if it is not there within a few minutes).
4. Open the link in the email and choose a new password.
5. Sign in again on your devices with the new password.

**Lost your phone or your second-factor app?** You cannot reset that yourself. Send a message to whoever invited you and they will reset it for you.
